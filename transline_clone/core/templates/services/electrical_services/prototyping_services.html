Tool & Die Design Engineering Services

Frequently Asked Questions

What is custom tooling?

Custom tooling includes specially designed tools, dies, fixtures, and gauges developed for specific manufacturing requirements.

What materials are used for tooling?

Tool steels, hardened alloys, stainless steel, carbide, and specialized engineering materials.

Why is prototype development important?

It helps validate product functionality, improve designs, and reduce manufacturing risks.

What is progressive tooling?

A manufacturing method where multiple operations are performed sequentially within a single tool.

What software do you use?

AutoCAD, UG NX, SolidWorks, Autodesk Inventor, 3D QuickPress, and CAD/CAM solutions.
