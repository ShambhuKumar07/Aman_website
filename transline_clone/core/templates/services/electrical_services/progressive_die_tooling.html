Progressive die tooling is a highly efficient metal stamping method used for producing precision components in high volumes. It involves a specially designed progressive die that performs multiple operations in a single press cycle as a metal strip moves through a series of workstations.

Each station carries out operations such as punching, blanking, bending, forming, or trimming. With every stroke of the press, the material advances through the tooling system until the final component is produced.

This integrated tooling system eliminates multiple setups, improves production speed, reduces handling time, and ensures consistent dimensional accuracy.

Progressive die tooling is widely used across automotive, electrical, electronics, and general engineering industries.

How Progressive Die Tooling Works

A highly efficient automated metal stamping process designed for precision, consistency, and high-volume production.

Progressive die tooling is a continuous metal stamping process used to produce precision components efficiently and in high volumes.

Read More

It works by feeding a metal strip from a coil into a stamping press fitted with a progressive die.

As the strip enters the die, it moves through a series of stations arranged in a fixed sequence. Each station performs a specific operation such as punching, blanking, bending, forming, or trimming.

With every stroke of the press, the material advances by a set distance, allowing the next operation to be carried out at the next station.

Multiple operations take place simultaneously on different sections of the strip. Gradually, the component takes shape as it moves through each stage of the die.

At the final station, the completed part is separated from the strip and ejected while the remaining material is removed for recycling.

This fully automated and integrated process ensures high production speed, excellent accuracy, minimal material waste, and consistent part quality.

This automated process delivers high productivity, lower labor requirements, improved manufacturing efficiency, and consistent product quality.
