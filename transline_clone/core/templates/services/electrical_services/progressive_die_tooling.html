What is Progressive Die Tooling?

Progressive die tooling is an advanced metal stamping technology used to manufacture high-precision components through a sequence of automated operations within a single die system.

As the metal strip moves through multiple stations, operations such as punching, blanking, forming, bending, and trimming are performed simultaneously, ensuring exceptional productivity and accuracy.

How Progressive Die Tooling Works

Progressive die tooling is a continuous metal stamping process used to manufacture precision components efficiently and in high volumes. A metal strip is fed from a coil into a stamping press equipped with a progressive die containing multiple workstations.

As the strip advances through each station, different operations such as punching, blanking, bending, forming, and trimming are performed. Multiple operations occur simultaneously during every press stroke, allowing the component to gradually take shape until the finished part is separated at the final station.

Manufacturing Benefits

This fully automated process ensures high production speed, excellent dimensional accuracy, reduced material waste, lower labor requirements, and consistent product quality, making progressive die tooling ideal for mass production.
